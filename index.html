FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How do I book a photography session?

Simply click the "Book Now" button on any package or use the contact form below. You can also reach us directly via WhatsApp at +254 713 541293 to discuss your requirements and available dates.

What areas do you cover?

We provide professional photography services across Kenya. We specialize in outdoor photography and can travel to your preferred location for the shoot.

How long does it take to receive my photos?

Edited photos are typically delivered within 7-14 days after your session. For urgent requests, we offer express delivery options at an additional cost.

Can I request specific poses or styles?

Absolutely! We encourage you to share your vision and any reference photos you like. We'll work together to create the perfect shots that match your style and preferences.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfers, and cash payments. A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the balance due on the day of the shoot.

Do you provide props and accessories?

Yes! We have a collection of props and accessories available for certain packages. Feel free to bring your own items as well to personalize your shoot.
